# Client Recommendations

## Client A – Startup Company
*Recommended Platform:* Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

*Explanation:* GCP is a strong fit for this startup because of its developer-friendly tools, competitive pricing, and generous free-tier/credits programs aimed at early-stage companies. Its serverless and managed services reduce the need for a large operations team, keeping costs low while the app is still small. As the user base grows, GCP's infrastructure (the same backbone used by Google Search and YouTube) can scale automatically to handle rapid increases in traffic without requiring major architecture changes.

*Services the client could use:*
- *App Engine* – to deploy and scale the mobile app's backend without managing servers.
- *Firebase* – for mobile app development, authentication, and real-time database needs.
- *Cloud Storage* – for storing user-generated content such as images and files.

## Client B – University
*Recommended Platform:* Microsoft Azure

*Explanation:* Since the university already runs on Microsoft's ecosystem, Azure is the natural choice because it integrates directly with Windows Server and Active Directory, minimizing the need to re-architect existing systems. Azure allows the university to extend its on-premises identity management into the cloud using Microsoft Entra ID, and staff/students already familiar with Microsoft 365 will have a smoother transition. Azure also offers education-specific pricing and compliance certifications relevant to academic institutions.

*Services the client could use:*
- *Azure Virtual Machines* – to migrate existing Windows Server workloads.
- *Microsoft Entra ID* – to extend Active Directory into the cloud for unified identity management.
- *Azure Files* – for shared file storage accessible across university departments.

## Client C – AI Research Company
*Recommended Platform:* Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

*Explanation:* GCP is best suited for AI/ML-heavy workloads because Google is a pioneer in machine learning research and offers some of the most advanced AI tooling available, including access to high-performance TPUs (Tensor Processing Units) built specifically for ML training. GCP's data analytics tools also make it easier to prepare and manage the large datasets typically required for AI research. This gives the company a technical edge that AWS and Azure's AI offerings don't match as closely.

*Services the client could use:*
- *Vertex AI* – for building, training, and deploying machine learning models.
- *Compute Engine with TPUs/GPUs* – for high-performance model training.
- *BigQuery* – for large-scale data processing and analysis to support research.

## Client D – Global E-Commerce Company
*Recommended Platform:* Amazon Web Services (AWS)

*Explanation:* AWS is the strongest choice for a global e-commerce platform due to its unmatched global infrastructure footprint of regions and availability zones, ensuring low latency and high availability for customers worldwide. AWS's auto-scaling and load-balancing services are mature and battle-tested at massive scale (AWS itself powers Amazon's own retail platform). Its wide range of services also supports everything from payment processing to inventory management and fraud detection, all within one ecosystem.

*Services the client could use:*
- *Amazon EC2 with Auto Scaling* – to automatically handle traffic spikes during sales events.
- *Amazon RDS* – for managing product, order, and customer databases reliably.
- *Amazon CloudFront* – to deliver content quickly to customers across the globe.
